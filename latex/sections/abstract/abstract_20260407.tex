%% ============================================================
%%  Abstract  (2026-04-07)
%%  Depositor Discipline in Modern Banking
%%  Target: < 100 words
%% ============================================================

We provide causal evidence that uninsured depositors discipline community banks
in response to credit-risk disclosures in normal times.  Exploiting the mandatory Current Expected Credit Loss (CECL) Day-One
retained-earnings adjustment as a predetermined information shock,
we find that banks with larger adjustments experienced contractions in uninsured
time deposits and higher funding costs after adoption.  A triple-difference specification confirms a within-bank composition
shift rather than a uniform funding retreat.  Financial penalties are amplified
at banks serving financially sophisticated depositors.  The results establish
that depositor discipline is an active governance mechanism at institutions
without implicit government guarantees.
