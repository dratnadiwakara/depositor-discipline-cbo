%% ============================================================
%%  Abstract  (2026-04-08)
%%  Depositor Discipline in Modern Banking
%%  Target: < 100 words
%% ============================================================

We provide causal evidence that uninsured depositors discipline community banks
in response to credit-risk disclosures, even outside periods of banking stress.
Exploiting the mandatory Current Expected Credit Loss (CECL) Day-One
retained-earnings adjustment as a predetermined information shock,
we find that banks with larger adjustments experienced contractions in uninsured
time deposits and higher funding costs after adoption.  The decline is confined
to uninsured deposits while insured balances are unaffected, confirming targeted
discipline rather than a uniform funding retreat.  Effects are amplified at
banks serving financially sophisticated depositors.  The results establish
that depositor discipline is an active governance mechanism at institutions
without implicit government guarantees.
