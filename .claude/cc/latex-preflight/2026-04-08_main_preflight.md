### Issue 1 — Confidence interval level not disclosed in event-study captions

- **Severity:** High
- **Category:** figure/table
- **Location:** `latex/sections/results/tables_figures.tex` lines 105, 175, 222 (captions for `fig:es_time_deps_cecl`, `fig:es_financial_outcomes_cecl`, `fig:es_sophistication_split`)
- **Problem:** All event-study captions describe the shaded band as "a pointwise confidence interval" without specifying the confidence level. Referees and readers will assume 95%.
- **Evidence:** `code/_common.R` line 22: `CI_Z <- 1.645` (90% z-score). The function `make_split_es_plot` in `_common.R` generates an in-figure subtitle that reads "90% confidence intervals shaded." The standalone event-study captions in `tables_figures.tex` have no such statement.
- **Suggested fix:** Add "90% confidence intervals shaded" to each of the three event-study captions, consistent with the figure subtitle already generated by `make_split_es_plot`. For the single-group figures (figs for time deps and financial outcomes), also add it explicitly.
